## Decisions

The lead maintainer makes final decisions. Changes that alter default numerical results, the
rules file or this governance document need an issue with at least 7 days for comment before they
merge. Everything else follows normal pull-request review.

Changes are judged against [RESPONSIBLE_USE.md](RESPONSIBLE_USE.md). A pull request that breaks
that policy is closed whatever its technical merit.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor with several merged pull requests and good judgment in reviews may be invited by
the lead maintainer. Maintainers who are inactive for a year are moved to emeritus.
