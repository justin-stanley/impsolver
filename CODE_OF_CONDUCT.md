# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion on the project's work, not on
people or politics.

Because the subject is politically charged, the project also asks contributors to follow
[RESPONSIBLE_USE.md](RESPONSIBLE_USE.md): no advocacy for or against any official or party in
issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability**
form and mark the report as a conduct report. The maintainers listed in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) will review it, keep the reporter's identity private, and respond
within 14 days. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction,
warning, temporary ban or permanent ban.
